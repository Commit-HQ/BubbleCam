# Changelog

What's new in each version of BubbleCam, newest first. The version a page was built from is in the footer of the landing pages and the cartoons' page.

## 0.3.1 (2026-11-17)

- In the settings, the advanced tools come right after the cartoon's shape.

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-17)

A voice for the toys.

- A cartoon can have a voice: a microphone beside the speeds records it while the cartoon plays through once, after a countdown of three. The same button ends the recording sooner, a new recording takes the old one's place, and a bin beside the microphone takes the voice away.
- The voice plays with the cartoon and is in its video, starting over each time a video plays the cartoon again.
- The browser asks for the camera and the microphone together the first time, so recording a voice doesn't stop to ask. The microphone is on only while a voice is recorded, and the privacy page says so.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-17)

Settings for grown-ups, and a button that's harder to press twice.

- The red button rests for a moment after each picture, so a child who presses twice gets one picture.
- Settings, behind the cog in the cartoons' page's header, open after a sum from the times table, which keeps them for grown-ups. The language moved there from the header.
- New cartoons can lie, stand, or follow how the device is held, and the screen can be kept from turning, where the browser can do it: Chrome on Android.
- How long the red button rests, the sounds, and the quality of pictures and of videos can each be set.
- Deleting a cartoon and making a video can each ask the same sum first.
- Advanced tools, off until a grown-up turns them on: zooming in, the ghost's strength, a grid over the camera, moving pictures by dragging them in the strip, holding a picture on screen for up to five seconds, and a video that plays the cartoon several times over.
- A self-timer among the advanced tools counts down three, five, or ten seconds before a picture, and can go on by itself, picture after picture, until the red button stops it.
- A cartoon's card shows its first picture full size, where it used to show a blurry thumbnail.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-02)

The first prototype.

- A studio for stop-motion cartoons: the camera with a ghost of the last picture over it, a big red button, a strip of the pictures taken, and a bin for the last one.
- Play a cartoon at three speeds, a turtle, footsteps, and a rabbit, and look back at any picture.
- Make a cartoon into an MP4 video to save or share, made on the device.
- The cartoons' page keeps every cartoon on the device, with a preview that plays its pictures.
- Works without the internet once it has been opened, and installs on phones, tablets, and computers.
- A landing page and a privacy page, in Croatian and English.
