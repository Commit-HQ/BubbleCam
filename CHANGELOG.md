# Changelog

What's new in each version of BubbleCam, newest first. The version a page was built from is in the footer of the landing pages and the cartoons' page.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-02)

The first prototype.

- A studio for stop-motion cartoons: the camera with a ghost of the last picture over it, a big red button, a strip of the pictures taken, and a bin for the last one.
- Play a cartoon at three speeds, a turtle, footsteps, and a rabbit, and look back at any picture.
- Make a cartoon into an MP4 video to save or share, made on the device.
- The cartoons' page keeps every cartoon on the device, with a preview that plays its pictures.
- Works without the internet once it has been opened, and installs on phones, tablets, and computers.
- A landing page and a privacy page, in Croatian and English.
